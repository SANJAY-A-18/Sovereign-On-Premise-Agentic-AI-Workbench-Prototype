Apex Institute of Technology - Central Learning Resource Centre (CLRC) Charter 2026
Document Reference: Charter LIB-POL-2026-V4
Effective Period: Academic Year 2026-2027
Supervising Authority: Mrs. Rajeshwari Ramanathan, Chief Librarian

1. Membership and Borrowing Allowances
All registered undergraduate students are issued an automated smart library credential upon admission. Under Section 1.4, undergraduates may borrow up to 4 volumes simultaneously for a lending duration of 16 calendar days. Faculty members and doctoral fellows may borrow up to 10 volumes for 45 calendar days. Reference dictionaries, thesis dissertations, and rare manuscripts housed in Wing C (2nd Floor) cannot be borrowed and must be inspected on-site using visitor badge CLRC-SEC-9.

2. Overdue Fines and Suspensions
Borrowers failing to return circulating materials by the due date will be assessed a overdue charge of Rs 15 per day per item for the initial 7 days of delinquency. Beginning on day 8, overdue fines escalate to Rs 40 per day until the item is returned. If an item remains overdue for more than 30 days, borrowing privileges are automatically suspended across all campus branch libraries.

3. Replacement Policy for Damaged or Lost Materials
In the event of accidental loss or irreparable mutilation of library books, the borrower is obligated to furnish an identical replacement copy of the same or newer edition within 21 calendar days, along with a mandatory administrative handling surcharge of Rs 600. If the book is out of print, the borrower must reimburse the library at 2.5 times the latest publisher catalogue list price.

4. Cyber Commons and Workstation Ethics
The Cyber Commons houses 60 high-performance computer terminals reserved for academic literature searches and research. User sessions are strictly capped at 90 minutes per student per day. Downloading pirated media or executing unauthorized peer-to-peer software triggers an immediate 30-day facility debarment and an infraction fine of Rs 3,200.

5. Examination Period Night Reading Hours
During mid-semester and end-semester testing intervals, the Ground Floor reading annex operates on an extended schedule closing at 02:30 AM. Entry after 10:00 PM requires biometric verification at Terminal Kiosk-L2.
