Apex Institute of Technology - End-Semester Examination Regulations (Gazette Ex-2026)
Document Reference: Regulation EXAM-REG-502
Issued by: Office of the Controller of Examinations
Authorized Officer: Dr. Hemant V. Deshmukh, Controller of Examinations

1. Hall Entry and Examination Timing Cutoffs
Candidates must arrive at designated examination halls 15 minutes before scheduled start time. Under Section 3.2, hall entry is strictly barred 20 minutes after the commencement bell rings. No candidate shall be permitted to submit their answer script or leave the hall during the first 45 minutes or the final 15 minutes of the session.

2. Grading and Passing Standard
To secure a passing grade in any theory course, a candidate must obtain at least 42 percent in the end-semester written paper and an aggregate of 50 percent combining continuous internal evaluation (CIE) and end-semester scores. For laboratory practicals, the standalone passing mark is 50 percent evaluated by an external examiner appointed by the Senate.

3. Supplementary Examinations
Students who fail up to two courses are entitled to sit for supplementary examinations scheduled within 28 calendar days following official result publication. A non-refundable registration fee of Rs 1,150 per theoretical credit must be remitted via the student portal before November 12, 2026.

4. Academic Malpractice Penalties
Under Clause 8.4, unauthorized possession of handwritten notes, programmable devices, or smart wearables constitutes a Level-2 academic infraction. The candidate's entire current examination series is cancelled immediately, accompanied by a mandatory 2-semester academic suspension. The Flying Squad Inspection team, led by Convener Prof. Sunita Chhabra, holds unrestricted inspection authority across all examination venues.

5. Script Re-Evaluation and Verification
Candidates seeking verification or double-blind re-evaluation of theory answer scripts must apply via Form RV-11 within 12 calendar days of score release. The fee is Rs 850 per course script. If the revised score differs by more than 15 percent, a third senior examiner evaluates the script, and the fee is refunded in full.
